# NOTICE — unofficial experimental project

**test12** is an **unofficial, non-commercial experimental** re-implementation
of a voxel sandbox survival game. It is a browser/web-technology study in
reproducing the general look-and-feel of Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.4.2
(Update Aquatic phase 1).

- NOT affiliated with / endorsed by Mojang Studios, Microsoft, or Minecraft.
- No code, textures, models, audio, or trademarks are copied from Minecraft or
  from commercial asset packs.
- Visual language is reproduced with original, runtime-generated, or
  license-compatible assets.
- "Minecraft" and related names are trademarks of their respective owners, used
  here only nominatively for compatibility description.

## Asset origin

- All voxel textures/colors in the Phase A scaffold are generated from code
  (solid lambert colors) — no image assets are bundled.
- Rendering uses the open-source **Three.js** library (MIT).
- Nothing in this repository is taken from the Minecraft installation or from
  commercial asset packs.
